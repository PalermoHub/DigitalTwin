# «Population trend 2011-2023» profile

## What the trend profile shows

Keywords: trend profile, change 2011-2021, UPL, neighbourhood, district, chart, projection 2030, 2035, band, trend, population change, population growth, population decline.

Typical questions: What does the trend profile show? What does the «Trend +5%» badge mean? What are the 2030 and 2035 points?

In the **«Population»** tab of the place profile the entry **«Population trend 2011-2023»** appears. It shows the three levels of the clicked point: the **UPL**, the **Neighbourhood** and the **District**. The badge is «Trend» followed by the percentage change 2011-2021 of the UPL.

- The group **«Change 2011-2021»** lists, for each level and with its name, the percentage change in inhabitants between 2011 and 2021.
- Below, a chart for each level: the observed points 2011, 2021 and 2023 and the points of the **trend scenario** 2030 and 2035, with an uncertainty band. The years are abbreviated (’11, ’21, ’23, ’30, ’35).

The colours follow the change: purple when the population falls, green when it grows. Hovering over an area shows the name and an «Inhabitants 2011-2021» badge with the change.

The profile gives the source (ISTAT, Census 2011, Permanent Census 2021 and 2023; processing by DigitalTwin Palermo, with the 2011 population distributed over the current areas by overlay) and a note of caution: **the 2011 census (traditional) and those of 2021 and 2023 (permanent, sample-based) are not fully comparable**, and the changes of a single area between 2021 and 2023 should be read with caution.
