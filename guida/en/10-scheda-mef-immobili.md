# Profile «Property declared to the MEF»

## What the profile of an MEF property shows

Keywords: MEF profile, declared property, asset, type, nature, area, volume, period of construction, cadastral identifier, use, purpose, restriction, legal status.

Typical questions: What does the profile of a public property show? What is the cadastral identifier? What does «approximate position» mean?

When you click on a building, a plot of land or a point of the «Properties declared to the MEF» layer, the profile has the title «Property declared to the MEF» and, as its badge, the census year (2023). For each asset there are these rows, when the data is present:

- **Type** and **Nature** of the asset.
- **Address**.
- **Area** in m² and **Volume** in m³.
- **Period of construction**.
- **Cadastral identifier** (sheet and parcel).
- **Use** and **Purpose**.
- **Cultural or landscape restriction**.
- **Legal status**.
- **Position**: says how precise it is. «Cadastral data» (the most reliable), «Address (street number)», «Street only: approximate position» or «Municipality only: approximate position».
- **Location method**: how the polygon of the asset was found. It can be Cadastre, Municipal properties, Schools, Polling stations, Municipal offices, Monuments or Declared position. If «(MEF position corrected)» appears, the position declared to the MEF contradicted the source and was corrected.

At the bottom are the source (Ministero dell'economia e delle finanze, Dipartimento del Tesoro, census of public properties, CC BY 4.0) and the «Open data of the public real estate census» link.

## Several assets in the same building

Keywords: several assets, list of assets, accordion, other declared assets, building with several properties.

Typical questions: What happens if a building has several properties? Why do I see only 12 assets?

If a building contains several assets, the profile shows the list with the number of declared assets. Each entry has the type and the address as a preview; selecting it shows the details. At most 12 assets are shown: if there are more, a row «Other declared properties» says how many are missing. If there is only one asset, the rows are shown directly.

## MEF property tooltips

Keywords: tooltip, hover box, declared assets, mouse hover.

Typical questions: What does the hover box of an MEF property show?

Hovering over a property shows «Property declared to the MEF» with the type (or nature) of the first asset, the number of declared assets and the address of the first asset.

Warning: the position of the assets is checked against the cadastre and the layers already mapped, but for some of the assets (about 2,400 out of 8,000) it is only the position declared to the MEF and cannot be verified. The data is informational.
