# «Water district» and «Nearby public transport» profiles

## «Water district» profile

Keywords: water district, AMAP, water, water network, district.

Typical questions: Which water district am I in? What is AMAP?

With the «AMAP water districts» layer (Boundaries group) a click on an area opens the section **«Water district»**, with the badge **AMAP** and a single row: **District**, the name of the district. Source: AMAP S.p.A., Water districts of Palermo.

## «Nearby public transport» profile

Keywords: nearby public transport, nearby stops, nearest stop, radius, as the crow flies, lines, stations.

Typical questions: Which stops are near me? How far does it search? Why can't I see all the stops?

In the **«Place»** tab, after a click on the map, the section **«Nearby public transport»** appears. It lists the stops nearest to the clicked point (at most 8), with the lines that serve them in the colour of each line; stations that are opening soon are also flagged. A tap on the name takes the map to the stop.

The note says: «Stops within [radius] m as the crow flies. Tap the name to see it on the map.» If there are more stops than those listed, it adds «[n] more further away not listed». The radius is the one adjustable from the «Selection on the map» legend, the same one used by the point layers. The nearby stops are highlighted on the map as «Nearby stop», with the icon of the vehicle.
