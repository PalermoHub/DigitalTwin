# Printing the map and the profile

## Printing the map

Keywords: print, print the map, A4 A3 A2 A1 A0 format, landscape, portrait, scale 1:1,000, current view, scale bar, legend, sources and attributions, PDF, paper size, printout.

Typical questions: How do I print the map? Which formats are there? How do I print to scale? What does the sheet contain?

The button with the printer (**«Print the current map»**) in the toolbar opens a small menu with four choices:
- **Scale**: «Current view (no scale)» or a fixed scale: **1:1,000, 1:2,000, 1:5,000, 1:10,000, 1:25,000**.
- **Format**: **A4, A3, A2, A1, A0**.
- **Orientation**: **Landscape** or **Portrait**.
- The **Print** button (disabled while it prepares the image).

The menu closes with **Esc** or by clicking outside. The browser then opens the print window, from which you can also save to PDF.

**What the sheet contains**
- The title **«Palermo Digital Twin»** and a line with the date, the **centre of the map** (latitude and longitude) and, depending on the case, «scale 1:[n]» or «zoom [n]».
- The **image of the map** you are looking at, with all the layers that are on.
- With a fixed scale, the **scale bar** in metres or kilometres and the note: «[length] · scale 1:[n] (print at 100%, without fitting to the page)».
- The **«Legend»**, only for the layers that are on (hidden or empty legends are not printed).
- **«Sources and attributions»**: the attributions of the maps, without repetitions.

**Current view or fixed scale.** With the «Current view» the map is printed as it is. With a fixed scale the app takes the map, out of sight, to the size of the print frame and to the zoom matching the scale (with rotation and tilt reset, seen from above), waits for the tiles to arrive (at most 12 seconds), captures the image and puts the map back as it was. The centre stays that of the view.

**Dimensions.** The sheet has 10 mm margins and reserves about 65 mm below the map for the title, scale, legend and sources. For each format the resolution is 192 dpi; for A0 it is reduced (96 dpi) so as not to exceed the browser's limits, and it is reduced further if the graphics card cannot handle large sizes.

**To really print to scale** the sheet must be printed at 100%, without «fit to page».

## Printing the place profile

Keywords: print the profile, extract of the map, print the data of a point, place profile, print a point.

Typical questions: Can I print the profile of a point? What is on the sheet?

The **«Print the profile»** button in the header of the place profile prints the profile. At the top of the sheet an **extract of the displayed map** («Extract of the displayed map») is added, with a circle on the point that was clicked. If the map image cannot be read, it is printed without the extract. After printing, the profile goes back to how it was.
