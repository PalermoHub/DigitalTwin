# «Monument» profile

## What the profile of a monument shows

Keywords: monument profile, photo, description, category, Tourism Portal, lost building, no longer existing, Municipality website, historic monuments.

Typical questions: What does the profile of a monument show? What does the «no longer existing» notice mean? Where do the texts come from?

When you click on a monument, the profile has the name as its title and the **category** as its badge. Below there are, when available:
- the **photo**;
- the **description**;
- the link **«Go to the Municipality website»** (Tourism Portal of the Municipality of Palermo);
- the source: «Tourism Portal — Municipality of Palermo», or the source given for that place.

If the description says that the building no longer exists (for example «non più esistente» (no longer existing), «demolito» (demolished), «abbattuto» (pulled down) or «distrutto» (destroyed)), the profile shows a **notice** of a lost building. If the description is only that phrase, it is not repeated as text.

The popup on the map shows title, category, photo, description, link and source. Where the monument has been matched to a building, the building is also coloured on the map; the buildings of lost monuments are not coloured.
