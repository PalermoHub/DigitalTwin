# «Works» tab and public works profiles

## Where the works are in the profile

Keywords: Works tab, works profile, public works, right-hand profile, works without a point on the map, summary.

Typical questions: Where do I find the details of a work? What is in the Works tab? How do I see the works that are not on the map?

The details of the public works are in the **Works** tab of the right-hand profile. The tab has three parts, in this order:
1. **Works without a point on the map**: a drop-down menu in two steps, «1 · Choose the owner» and «2 · Choose the work». It is for works that have no usable address (roads, scattered works, «municipal territory»…): it says how many works are on the map out of the total and lets you open the profile of any of them anyway. The badge of the title is the number of works in the menu.
2. **The profile of the work clicked** (one for each work chosen on the map).
3. **«Public works in Palermo»**: the summary with numbers and charts on all the works. The badge is the year the data was extracted.

The hover box on grouped markers invites you to click «for the list and more information»; the popup points to the profile, Works tab.

## The profile of a single work

Keywords: owner, other owners, CUP status, phase, sector, subsector, type, nature, category, CUP, OpenCUP, description.

Typical questions: What does «Phase (from declared dates)» mean? What is the CUP status? How do I see the CUP on OpenCUP?

The profile has the title of the work as its title (or the CUP, if it is missing) and the works phase as its badge. The rows, when the data exists:

**Data of the work**
- **Owner** (a click filters the map on the works of that owner) and **Other owners**.
- **CUP status**: ATTIVO (active) or CHIUSO (closed).
- **Phase (from declared dates)**: In use, Works completed, Execution finished, In execution, In design or Phase not stated. The source only says whether the CUP is active or closed: **the phase is derived from the actual dates declared by the owner**.
- **Sector**, **Subsector**, **Type**, **Nature**, **Category**.
- **Address** and **Position**: «Address (street number)» or «Street only: approximate position».
- **CUP**: the value is a link to the project page on OpenCUP.

**Declared costs and funding** (items that are zero or missing are not shown; 0 means «not stated» in the source): planned and actual works cost, planned contingency sums, planned investment charges, state, European, local authority and private funding, other sources, funding still to be found, savings.

**Dates**: actual design end; planned and actual works start and end; planned and actual completion end; actual start of use.

Below appears, if different from the title, the description of the work, then the links: «MOP Public Works projects on Open BDAP», «Open BDAP portal (location of the works)» and «Palermo public works on Cruscotto Italia». At the bottom the source: Open BDAP, Ragioneria generale dello Stato, Monitoraggio Opere Pubbliche (MOP), CC BY citing the source.

## The sectors of the works and the colours

Keywords: sectors, social infrastructure, transport, environment, energy, education, telecommunications, research, community services, marker colour.

Typical questions: What sectors are there? What do the marker colours mean?

The markers of the works are ring-shaped and the colour shows the sector. The ten sectors: Social infrastructure, Transport infrastructure, Environment and water resources, Energy, Productive activities and research, Productive areas, Education and training, Telecommunications and IT, Research and innovation, Community services. The legend filters by sector, by works with a closed CUP and by owner.

Hovering over a work, the hover box shows the title (shortened), phase and CUP status, owner, planned works cost and address. A marker can group several works at the same point («N public works» or «Several addresses at the same spot»).

## The «Public works in Palermo» summary

Keywords: works summary, totals, charts, works by sector, by phase, by CUP status, by year, by type, by owner, download CSV.

Typical questions: How many works are there? How much do they cost in total? Can I download the list?

The summary shows three boxes: **Works** (number), **Planned works cost** and **Completed or in use** (percentage). Then it says how many works are on the map out of the total («the others have no usable address and are in the full list»). The charts:
- Works and planned cost by sector.
- Works phase (with the note that the phase is derived from the declared dates).
- CUP status.
- Works by planned completion year (with a note on the works with no date).
- By type.
- By owner (a click on an owner filters the map on its works; if some works are not on the map it says so).

At the bottom there is the link to download the full list (CSV) with the extraction date.
