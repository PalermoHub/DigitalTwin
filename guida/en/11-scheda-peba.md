# Profile «PEBA – Architectural barriers»

## What the PEBA profile shows

Keywords: PEBA profile, details, accessibility, level, inaccessibility score, relevance, priority class, estimated cost, district average, PDF sheet.

Typical questions: What does «Partially inaccessible» mean? What is the inaccessibility score? Where do I download the original sheet?

When you click on an element of the «Architectural barriers» layer, the profile has the title «PEBA – Architectural barriers» and, as its badge, the accessibility level. Below the title appear the district, neighbourhood and UPL. The groups of rows:

**Details**
- **Code** (coloured like the group), **Name**, **Address**.
- **Estimated cost** of the interventions, if given.
- **Group**, **Category**, **Urban context**, **Element**.

**Accessibility**
- **Level**: Accessible, Partially accessible, Partially inaccessible, Inaccessible or Not assessable.
- **Inaccessibility score**: from 0 to 100 (the higher it is, the more inaccessible the place), also shown with a bar.
- **Relevance** (for example High) and **Priority class** (Class 1, 2…).
- **District average**: the average score of the district, with the difference of the place from the average (for example «52 (+8)»).

## Critical issues, photos and PDF sheet

Keywords: critical issues found, survey photos, viewer, thumbnails, original PDF, rotate photos.

Typical questions: Where do I see the critical issues? Can I enlarge the photos? Where do I find the PDF?

Below the rows the profile draws the score bar, the list of the **critical issues found** (separated by semicolons in the source) and the **survey photos**. The photos are thumbnails: a click opens the **viewer**, which lets you scroll through the photos (in a loop) and rotate them. The large photos are on the PEBA site of PalermoHub.

At the bottom there are two links:
- **Open original PDF sheet**: the PDF of the survey, with the file size (some are heavy);
- **More information about the PEBA**: the presentation page of the plan on PalermoHub.

Below appears the source: Comune di Palermo, PEBA (Giunta resolution no. 272 of 18/07/2026), processed by OpenDataSicilia and PalermoHub, CC BY 4.0.

## PEBA colours and groups on the map

Keywords: PEBA legend, groups, levels, colours, school, nursery, square, museum, historic route, UNESCO site.

Typical questions: What types of places are in the PEBA? How do I change the colour between level and group?

The PEBA legend works as a filter and also chooses the colour of the map: by **Accessibility** (level) or by **Group**.
- **Levels**: Accessible, Partially accessible, Partially inaccessible, Inaccessible, Not assessable.
- **Groups**: Historic route, City street, School, Nursery, Administrative/institutional office, Green area, UNESCO site (building), UNESCO route, Square, Museum.

Buildings and green areas are polygons, the rest are points with an icon for each group. Hovering shows the code, the name and a badge with the level (or the group, depending on the mode chosen).

## Reliability of the PEBA data

Keywords: PEBA errors, geocoding, automatic extraction, inaccurate positions.

Typical questions: Can I trust the position and the texts of the PEBA?

Profiles, texts and photos were extracted automatically from the original PDFs of the survey with Python scripts and the assistance of an artificial intelligence model; the coordinates derive from the geocoding of the addresses. There may be errors, missing fields or inaccurate placements. For official data the original PDF of the Comune applies.
