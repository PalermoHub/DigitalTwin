# «Commuting for work» profile

## What the commuting profile shows

Keywords: commuting profile, inbound, outbound, balance, live and work in Palermo, ranking of municipalities, commuters 2021, remote working, commuters, work travel.

Typical questions: What does «Balance» mean? What are inbound and outbound? Why is the 2021 data special?

In the **«Population»** tab of the place profile the entry **«Commuting for work»** appears, with the badge **2021**. If you clicked on a municipality, there is a group with the name of the municipality and three rows:
- **Inbound (work in Palermo)**: people who live in that municipality and work in Palermo.
- **Outbound (Palermo residents)**: residents of Palermo who work in that municipality.
- **Balance (inbound − outbound)**, with the sign + or −.

Below, the profile draws a summary and rankings: how many live and work in Palermo, how many leave, how many come in, the balance and the shares; the ranking of the linked municipalities (the top 25 links, if they weigh at least 1% of the total of the direction) and the municipalities with the largest balance.

On the map, hovering over Palermo shows «Live and work here» with the number; over a municipality it shows the **Balance**, in blue if positive (more people come in) and orange if negative.

Source and cautions written in the profile: ISTAT, Permanent Census 2021, commuting-for-work matrix (CC BY 4.0), original map by PalermoHub. **Data at 31 December 2021, in the middle of the pandemic: the movements may reflect remote working. The municipalities are represented by their centroid and the arc is not the real route.** There is a link to the original «Commuting» map.
