# Territory, cadastre and urban planning

## Cadastre: the cadastral parcels

Keywords: cadastre, parcel, sheet, number, land registry search, cadastral extract, SITR, Agenzia delle Entrate, property boundary.

Typical questions: How do I find a parcel? What does the cadastre layer show? Can I get the cadastral extract?

The «Cadastre: parcels» layer (Territory group, visible from zoom 12) shows the cadastral parcels of Palermo: about 243,700, from the SITR of the Sicilian Region and the Agenzia delle Entrate (September 2026). The labels «F. [foglio] - P. [particella]» appear from zoom 15 (16 on a smartphone), with the «Sheet and parcel» switch in the legend.

A click opens the profile: in the «Planning tools» section the parcel appears with sheet and number and the link to the cadastral extract. To search for one directly, use the «Filters» tab (sheet and number) or the search field.

The cadastral data are informational only: they do not replace official extracts or certificates.

## Zoning plan (PRG 2004): zoning and PPE

Keywords: PRG, zoning plan, zoning, ZTO, zone A B C D E F, PPE, historic centre, historic core, land-use designation.

Typical questions: What planning zone is this point in? What is the PPE? Is the zoning plan up to date?

The «Zoning plan 2004: zoning and PPE» layer shows the 2004 General Variant to the zoning plan: the homogeneous territorial zones (ZTO) and the Executive Detailed Plan (PPE) of the historic centre. For the historic centre the profile indicates the area (historic centre, historic core) and the instrument (PPE).

The legend of the PRG and of the PPE is in the panel. Data from the Municipality of Palermo, reworked by OpenDataSicilia (CC BY 4.0).

Warning: this is the 2004 general variant. Later variants may not be included. For legal uses the certificate of urban planning designation is needed.

## PRG 2004 constraints

Keywords: constraints, area constraints, linear constraints, protection, buffer zones.

Typical questions: Are there constraints on this area? Where do I see the constraints?

The «Zoning plan 2004: constraints» layer shows the area and linear constraints of the zoning plan. A click on a point lists the overlapping constraints in the «Planning tools» section of the profile, next to the zoning. They are informational data, with no legal value.

## OMI zones and property quotations

Keywords: OMI, quotations, house prices, rent, sale, €/m², Agenzia delle Entrate, property market, band.

Typical questions: How much does a house cost in this area? What is an OMI zone?

The «OMI zones 2025» layer (second half of 2025) shows the zones of the Osservatorio del Mercato Immobiliare of the Agenzia delle Entrate. The profile, in the «Market» section, gives zone, band with description, micro-zone and the quotation ranges: sale in €/m² and rent in €/m² per month, by type. They are indicative quotations, not transaction prices or valuations.

## Municipal properties

Keywords: municipal properties, municipal assets, municipal buildings, automatic georeferencing.

Typical questions: Which properties does the Municipality own? Are the positions exact?

The «Municipal properties» section of the Territory group shows the properties of the Municipality of Palermo (2024 data). The positions are the result of automatic georeferencing and are purely indicative. For the public assets declared to the Ministry of Economy and Finance see also the «Properties declared to the MEF» layer.

## PAI constraints: hydrogeological risk

Keywords: PAI, landslide, flood, hazard, hydraulic risk, geomorphological, instabilities, coastal erosion, attention sites, River Basin Authority.

Typical questions: Is this area at risk of landslide or flood? What is the PAI?

The «PAI plan» group presents the Hydrogeological Assessment Plan of the Sicilian Region for the Municipality of Palermo: the 12 themes of the plan: hydraulic hazard and risk, hydraulic attention sites, floods (discharge manoeuvre and collapse), geomorphological hazard and risk, geomorphological attention sites, P3 and P4 buffer zone, instabilities (by activity and by type), coastal erosion hazard and risk, with the official symbology of the server.

A click on an area opens the profile: in the «Planning tools» section the «PAI constraints» box appears with the most severe class among those overlapping. They are informational data: for legal uses the official cartography of the River Basin Authority applies.

## Fires

Keywords: fires, burnt areas, fire, Bellolampo, Fire Census, wooded area.

Typical questions: Where have there been fires? How many hectares have burnt?

The «Fires» layer (Events group, from zoom 12) shows the areas swept by fire in the Municipality of Palermo since 2007, year by year, from the Fire Census of the Sicilian Region. The colour indicates the year. The profile of a fire gives date, locality, total and wooded area and, when available, the crews that intervened. If several fires overlap, the profile lists them all. In the search you can filter by year (for example «incendi 2023»).

## Heat islands

Keywords: heat islands, temperature, heat, Landsat, LST, summer, surface, census sections.

Typical questions: Where is it hottest in the city? Is the temperature the air temperature?

The «Heat islands» layer (Territory group, from zoom 10) shows the summer surface temperature obtained from the Landsat 8 and 9 satellites (USGS) for the 3,600 census sections of 2021; the map shows the year 2025. **It is the temperature of the surface, not of the air.**

The legend lets you choose the classification method (Jenks, quantiles, equal intervals) and from 3 to 9 classes, and shows the chart of the municipal trend from 2019 to 2025. The profile has the «Heat island» entry: summer temperature, difference from the municipal average and change since 2019. The link leads to the full PalermoHub study. Data: Landsat (public domain) processed by ISTAT section; OpenDataSicilia / PalermoHub study (CC BY 4.0).

## Terrain relief

Keywords: relief, DTM, altitude, elevation, hillshade, slope, terrain.

Typical questions: How do I see the relief? What elevation does this point have? What is the DTM?

The «Terrain» group has two layers: «3D terrain with hillshade» and «Elevation (coloured raster)». They are based on the 5-metre digital terrain model (HR-DTM-5m, IRPI-CNR, Panza et al. 2026, PalermoHub processing). The place profile includes a «Terrain (DTM 5 m)» entry with elevation, slope and morphometric indices.

## Administrative boundaries

Keywords: boundaries, districts, neighbourhoods, UPL, first-level units, census sections, AMAP, water districts.

Typical questions: Which district am I in? What is a UPL? How many census sections are there?

The «Boundaries» group has five layers: Districts (8, switched on at start-up), Neighbourhoods (25), UPL (55), AMAP water districts and Census sections (from zoom 13, the 2021 ISTAT ones). The UPL (first-level unit) is a subdivision of the Municipality finer than the neighbourhood. Data ISTAT / Municipality of Palermo (2021).
