# Services and culture

## Monuments and historic places

Keywords: monuments, churches, palaces, theatres, museums, Teatro Massimo, Tourism Portal, places of interest, categories.

Typical questions: Where are the historic churches? How do I see the profile of a monument? Which categories are there?

The «Monuments (Tourism Portal)» layer (Monuments group) shows about 4,000 places of historical and cultural interest. There are two sources: the Tourism Portal of the Municipality of Palermo («Cosa Vedere») and the «Monumental map of Palermo and the Palermo countryside» by Marcello Petrucci, merged without duplicates. Where it was possible to match them, the building is also coloured on the map.

Categories (which can be filtered in the legend): Churches and Oratories, Monuments, Theatres, Historic residences and villas, Palaces, Art galleries and museums, Libraries, Gardens and green spaces, Historic markets, Archaeological areas, Shelters and war memorials, Other places. Each category has its own icon.

A click opens the profile with photo, description, category and the link to the Tourism Portal, from which the texts come. Some monuments no longer exist: they are flagged in the profile («no longer existing»). Texts and photos belong to the Municipality of Palermo and to the author of the map.

## Monumental trees

Keywords: trees, monumental trees, MASAF, species, circumference, height, ficus.

Typical questions: Where are the monumental trees? How many are there?

The «Monumental trees (MASAF)» layer (Monuments group) shows about 46 monumental trees of Palermo from the list of the Ministry of Agriculture (MASAF). The profile gives species, locality, altitude, circumference, height, the monumentality criteria and the proposal for a declaration of notable public interest. Details in `14-scheda-alberi-monumentali.md`.

## Public drinking fountains

Keywords: drinking fountains, water, drink, fountain, drinking water.

Typical questions: Where do I find a drinking fountain? How many are there?

The «Public drinking fountains» layer (Monuments group) shows about 150 public drinking fountains with type of location, name, address, UPL, neighbourhood and district, and the link to the in-depth pages on PalermoHub. In the legend they are shown with the drop icon.

## Municipal schools and nurseries

Keywords: schools, nurseries, school buildings, institutes, school, children.

Typical questions: Where are the schools? How many municipal nurseries are there?

In the «Roadside services» group, the «Schools and polling sections» section has the «Municipal schools and nurseries» layer: about 271 points among nurseries, school buildings and institute offices, with the mortarboard icon. Open data of the Municipality of Palermo (2017): for this reason some sites may have changed. Each point is matched, where possible, to the building.

## Polling section sites

Keywords: polling sections, polling stations, vote, elections, where do I vote, polling station.

Typical questions: Where is my polling station? How many sections are there in one site?

The «Polling sections (sites)» layer (same section as the schools) shows the voting sites (132 in the data: 75 coincide with a school and are merged with the school, 57 are separate) with the list of the sections they host; the icon is the ballot in the box. Open data of the Municipality (2017): for your own polling station the electoral card always applies.

## Municipal offices

Keywords: offices, Municipality, areas, sectors, managers, contacts, Palazzo Palagonia, PEC, site.

Typical questions: Where is office X? Who is in charge? How do I contact an office?

The «Municipal offices (sites)» layer (Roadside services group) shows the 81 sites of the offices of the Municipality of Palermo. The more offices a site has, the bigger the ring. The profile of a site lists areas, sectors and operational units with managers, phone numbers, emails and PEC, when published, with the link to the office page. You can filter by area from the legend. Data from the Municipality's institutional website (extraction 3 October 2026): for official contacts the Municipality's website prevails.

## Charging points for electric cars

Keywords: charging points, charging, electric cars, EV, power, operator, status, direct current, alternating current.

Typical questions: Where do I charge my electric car? Is the charging point free?

The «Charging points» layer (Roadside services group) shows about 250 charging points with live status, power and operator. The status («Available», «Charging», «Inactive») is read live, with a fallback file updated every hour; «in use» is seen only for the charging points with real-time status; the legend has filters by status and type of current. The information on availability is indicative and depends on the data source.

## Fuel stations and prices

Keywords: fuel stations, petrol stations, gas stations, filling stations, petrol, gasoline, unleaded, diesel, LPG, methane, CNG, white pumps, independent stations, prices, brand, fuel, Osservaprezzi, MIMIT.

Typical questions: Where is petrol cheapest? Which stations sell methane?

The «Fuel stations» layer (Roadside services group) shows about 216 stations in Palermo with brand, address, fuels sold and prices reported by the operators (petrol, diesel, LPG, methane). The icon is the pump, in the colour of the brand. The legend filters by brand and by fuel. The data come from the MIMIT Osservaprezzi carburanti, regenerated every day (IODL 2.0 licence). The prices are those reported by the operators and may not match those at the pump.
