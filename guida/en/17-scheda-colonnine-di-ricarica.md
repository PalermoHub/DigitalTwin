# Profile «Charging points»

## What the charging points profile shows

Keywords: charging points profile, charging points, EV charging, operator, power kW, current, connector, opening hours, status, available, real time, list.

Typical questions: How do I see the nearby charging points? What does «Not updated in real time» mean? What does «Always open» mean?

In the place profile, the charging points near the clicked point are gathered in a single entry, **«Charging points»**, which has the number of charging points as its badge. The summary says how many there are and how many are «available». Above the list there are the charts for all the charging points of the Comune.

The list is an accordion: «Select a charging point to see the details». Each entry has the address (or the operator) as its title and the status as a preview. When opened, it shows the rows, when the data exists:
- **Operator**, **Address**.
- **Power** in kW with the type of current in brackets.
- **Connector** and the number of **Connectors**.
- **Opening hours**: «Always open (24 hours a day)» for the 24-hour charging points.
- **Status**: if the charging point is not updated in real time by the operator, the profile says «Not updated in real time by the operator».

The popup on the map shows operator, status, address, power, connector and number of connectors.

At the bottom there is the «Charging points in Sicily» link (EVChargingLogSicilia of PalermoHub: usage, trends and forecasts). The source also gives the date of the last status update: GSE, Piattaforma Unica Nazionale (PUN), through PalermoHub/evcharginglogsicilia, CC BY 4.0.
