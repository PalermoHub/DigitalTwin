# Header and controls of the place profile

## The profile header

Keywords: profile header, coordinates, district, neighbourhood, UPL, profile title, place profile, tabs.

Typical questions: Where do I see the coordinates of the point? How do I know which neighbourhood I am in? Why does the profile title change?

At the top of the profile there are:
- the **title** (that of the main section of the point; if there is none, «Place profile»);
- the **coordinates** of the point clicked, in degrees with five decimals, for example «38.11500° N, 13.36100° E»;
- the territorial **context**: «District [name]», «Neighbourhood [name]» and «UPL [name]» (only those available);
- the **tabs**, with arrows to scroll them right and left. Home and End go to the first and the last, the keyboard arrows move from one to the next. If a tab has more than one section, a number next to its name says how many there are.

On a smartphone the profile rises from the bottom like a sheet; a handle changes the height of the profile.

## Profile controls: copy, print, customise, close

Keywords: copy address, cadastral references, print the profile, customise the information, close the profile, Esc.

Typical questions: How do I copy the address and the parcel? How do I print the profile? How do I hide information I do not need?

At the top right of the profile there are four buttons:
- **Copy address and cadastral references**: copies the address and «Sheet [n], parcel [n]» to the clipboard. The button shows «Copied» for a moment.
- **Print the profile**: the printout includes an extract of the map as displayed.
- **Customise the information**: opens the «Customise» panel.
- **Close the profile** (also with Esc).

The **Customise** panel lets you choose which topics to show in the profiles: there is the «Search topic…» field, and the «Select all» and «Deselect all» buttons. The choices are saved in this browser; if the browser does not save them, they hold as long as the page stays open. **Warning:** the choice does not turn the layer on the map on, only the information in the profile; the layer has to be turned on by hand. If all the information is hidden the profile says: «All the information in this profile is hidden: open «Customise» to show it.»

## Layer switch, missing data and classes from 1 to 5

Keywords: show the layer on the map, hide the layer, no data at this point, not available, class out of 5, no data.

Typical questions: Why does the profile say «No data at this point»? What does «Class 3 of 5» mean?

- Each section of the profile has a switch, **«Show the layer on the map»** / **«Hide the layer from the map»**, to turn the layer of that topic on or off.
- If there is no data at the point the profile says «No data at this point: zoom in or turn on a layer.»
- When several rows of a section have no data, they are gathered in a single row «Not available: [list]».
- Rows with a judgement in classes (for example stability and buildability of the ground) show «Class [n] of 5 (1 = best)».
- Sections marked as informational carry the note «Informational data, with no legal value: for legal use, land-registry extracts and a certificate of planning use are required.»

## The disclaimer at the bottom of the profile

Keywords: disclaimer, legal notice, legal value, SISTER, certificate of planning use, sample-based estimates.

Typical questions: What does the notice at the bottom of the profile say?

At the bottom of every profile there is the notice: «The Digital Twin is a tool for learning, studying and understanding the city. It does not replace official documents. Cadastre, zoning plan and constraints are purely informational here and have no legal value. For a land-registry extract or a certificate of planning use, contact SISTER or the competent offices. Population data for individual buildings are also sample-based estimates and should be read as indications, not exact counts.»
