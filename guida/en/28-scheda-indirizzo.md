# «Address» profile

## What the Address profile shows

Keywords: address profile, street, house number, street number, suffix, nearest house number, Place tab, how to find an address.

Typical questions: How do I find out the address of a point? What does a house number with a slash, like 12/A, mean?

When you click on the map, in the **«Place»** tab the section **«Address»** appears, with two rows:
- **Street**: the name of the road (odonym).
- **Number**: the house number; if it has a suffix it is written with a slash, for example «12/A».

The address is that of the **nearest house number** to the clicked point among those of the «House numbers» layer (Municipality of Palermo, February 2026). It is a reference address for the point, not the house number of the building you clicked.
