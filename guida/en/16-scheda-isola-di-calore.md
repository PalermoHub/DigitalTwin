# Profile «Heat island»

## What the Heat island profile shows

Keywords: heat island profile, summer temperature, compared with the city average, change, Landsat, trend chart, census section, urban heat island, hot spot.

Typical questions: What does «Compared with the city average» mean? Since when is the change calculated? Is it the air temperature?

When you click on a section of the «Heat islands» layer (or on a point that falls in a section with data), the profile has the title **«Heat island»** and, as its badge, the summer temperature in °C. It is in the «Terrain» tab of the place profile. The rows:
- **Summer temperature**: value in °C with the year in brackets (the latest year available, 2025).
- **Compared with the city average**: the difference in °C between the section and the average of the Comune, with the sign (+ hotter, − cooler).
- **Change since 2019**: the difference in °C between the temperature of the latest year and that of the first year of the series (2019), if available.

Below the rows the profile draws a trend chart. At the bottom there is the «Full study on heat islands» link (PalermoHub: maps by year, bivariate and «true island» purged of the effect of the territory).

The source is written in the profile: Landsat 8/9 (USGS), summer land surface temperature, processed by ISTAT census section. **It is not the air temperature.**
