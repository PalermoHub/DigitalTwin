# «Fuel stations» profile

## What the fuel stations profile shows

Keywords: fuel stations profile, petrol, diesel, LPG, methane, self-service, serviced, prices of, other fuels, operator, brand, nearby fuel stations, fuel prices, petrol stations.

Typical questions: How do I see the prices of nearby fuel stations? What do «self» and «serviced» mean? What date are the prices from?

In the place profile, the fuel stations near the clicked point are gathered in one entry **«Fuel stations»** with the number as its badge. The list is an accordion: «Select a station to see the prices». Each entry has as its title the brand (or the operator) and as a preview the distance from the point. When opened, it shows:
- **Operator** and **Address**.
- **Petrol**, **Diesel**, **LPG**, **Methane (CNG)**: for each one the self-service and/or serviced price, with three decimals.
- **Other fuels**: the other types sold, with the minimum price.
- **Prices of**: the day the prices refer to.

The nearby fuel stations enter the profile within the radius adjustable from the «Selection on the map» legend and are highlighted on the map. The popup shows brand, address, prices of the four fuels and the day of the prices.

At the bottom there is the link «Find the lowest prices on Osservaprezzi carburanti» (the MIMIT site with prices by area). Source: MIMIT, Osservaprezzi carburanti (register of the stations and prices communicated by the operators), IODL 2.0. The prices are those communicated by the operators.
