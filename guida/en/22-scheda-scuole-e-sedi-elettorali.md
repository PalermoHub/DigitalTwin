# «Schools and nurseries» and «Polling station» profiles

## Profile of a school or nursery

Keywords: school profile, nursery, school building, institute, site, polling station, sections, school.

Typical questions: What does the profile of a school show? What does «Polling station» mean inside the profile of a school?

When you click on a point of the «Municipal schools and nurseries» layer, the profile has the name as its title and the type as its badge. The rows, when the data exists:
- **Institute** (the category).
- **Site**, with the address of the site if different.

If there is also a polling station in the same building, the profile adds a group **«Polling station»** with the list of the sections (the number and the list). The popup on the map adds **Address** and **Neighbourhood**.

A building that is also a school, a polling station or a monument shows in the profile an entry **«Building»** with the row **Use**: «School or nursery», «Polling station» or «Monument».

## Profile of a polling station

Keywords: polling station profile, polling place, electoral sections, district, voting.

Typical questions: Which sections vote at this polling station?

When you click on a point of the «Polling sections (sites)» layer, the profile has the name of the site as its title, the voting icon and the type as its badge. The group **«Polling station»** gives the number of sections and the list of the sections hosted. The popup on the map adds **Address** and **District**.

The polling stations that coincide with a school are shown in the profile of the school («Polling station» group). The data is the open data of the Municipality of Palermo from 2017.
