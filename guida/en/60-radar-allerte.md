# Rain radar and weather and flood alerts

## The "Rain radar" and "Weather and flood alert in Sicily" layers

Keywords: radar, rain, alert, weather alert, yellow alert, orange, red, Civil Protection, DPC, thunderstorms, landslide risk, flood risk, bad weather, weather.

Typical questions: Is it raining in Palermo? Is there a weather alert? Where can I see the rain radar? What does yellow alert mean?

In the Layers panel, in the **"Rain radar and alerts"** group, there are two layers you switch on separately. **"Rain radar"** shows where it is raining right now: the last 2 hours of radar, in 10-minute steps, as an animation you can pause with the button on the left of the slider and scrub by hand with the slider. From light blue (light rain) to red and purple (very heavy). When the service provides them, **forecast** images appear too, marked with the word "forecast" next to the time. **"Weather and flood alert in Sicily"** colours Sicily's **9 alert zones** by the level set by Civil Protection: green (no alert), **yellow**, **orange**, **red**.

## How to read the alerts

Keywords: alert zone, today, tomorrow, bulletin, criticality, thunderstorms, landslide, flood, profile, click.

Typical questions: Which alert zone is Palermo in? How do I see tomorrow's alert? What is in the alert profile?

Alert zones **do not follow municipal boundaries**: Palermo is in the **"Nord-Occidentale e isole Egadi e Ustica"** zone, which also includes, for example, Trapani and Monreale. In the legend the **"Today"** and **"Tomorrow"** buttons choose the day that colours the map; below them you find the Palermo zone and, if any, the Sicilian zones under alert with the risks that raised the level (thunderstorms, flood risk, landslide risk). Click a zone, even outside Palermo and with the layer off, and the place profile shows the **"Weather and flood alert"** card in the **"Place"** tab, with today's and tomorrow's level, the bulletin, the publication time and a link to the Civil Protection website.

## Updates, sources and limits

Keywords: update, 4 pm bulletin, RainViewer, Civil Protection, licence, CC BY, accuracy, official value.

Typical questions: How often does it update? Where does the data come from? Can I rely on it for decisions?

The Department of Civil Protection **criticality bulletin** (CC BY 4.0 licence) is normally published **by 4:00 pm** every day, with updates when the Regions correct it: a service (Worker) reads it and refreshes it every 15 minutes. The **radar** comes from **RainViewer**, which builds it from national weather radars and refreshes it every 10 minutes; it is free for personal, educational and small-community use and must be credited. The layer is **informational**: for safety decisions the official notices of Civil Protection and the Sicilian Region prevail. Radar sees rain in the air, not what reaches the ground, and mountains can shadow some areas.
