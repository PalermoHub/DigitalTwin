# Profile «Drinking fountain»

## What the profile of a drinking fountain shows

Keywords: drinking fountain profile, drinking water, address, location, name, notes, neighbourhood, district, AMAP, water fountain.

Typical questions: What does the profile of a drinking fountain show? Who does the data come from? Where do I find the map of the drinking fountains?

When you click on a point of the «Public drinking fountains» layer, the profile has the title **«Drinking fountain»** and the badge **«Drinking water»**. The rows, when the data exists:
- **Address**.
- **Location** (the type of location).
- **Name**.
- **Notes**.

The popup on the map adds **Neighbourhood** and **District** and, as a subtitle, the UPL (or the neighbourhood).

At the bottom there is the «For more details see the drinking fountains map» link, which leads to the map of the drinking fountains and of walking coverage on PalermoHub. Source: AMAP S.p.A., public drinking fountains of Palermo. In the place profile the section is in the services group (same weight as the charging points).
