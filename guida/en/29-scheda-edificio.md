# «Building» profile

## What the Building profile shows

Keywords: building profile, height, use, residents estimate, UNK, volumetric unit, building data.

Typical questions: What does the profile of a building show? What does «Use: UNK» mean? How is the number of residents calculated?

When you click on a building, the section **«Building»** appears in the **«Place»** tab and, copied, also at the start of the **«Planning tools»** tab. The rows, when the data exists:
- **Height** in metres (one decimal).
- **Use**: the intended use present in the data. If the value is «UNK» (not known) it is considered weak: when in the same building there is a monument, a school or a polling station, the profile shows **«Monument»**, **«School or nursery»** or **«Polling station»** instead.
- **Residents (estimate)**: estimated number of residents, rounded.

The figure is an **estimate**: the population per building is not a count. The polygons of the buildings are the volumetric units of the Carta Tecnica Comunale (Municipality of Palermo).
