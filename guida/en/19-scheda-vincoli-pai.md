# «PAI constraints» profile

## What the PAI constraints profile shows

Keywords: PAI profile, PAI constraints, hazard class, risk class, overlapping constraints, most severe, element at risk, measures, River Basin Authority, hydrogeological plan, flood hazard.

Typical questions: What does «most severe» mean? What are the measures? Why are there several constraints at the same point?

When you click on an area of a layer in the «PAI plan» group, the profile shows the constraint. The title is that of the theme (for example hydraulic hazard) and the badge is the class (the letter or number of the class, if the theme has one). The rows depend on the theme and are only there when the data exists: the classes of the theme, the description of the element, the **Area** in hectares and the **Length** in metres. A group **«Measures»** gathers, when there are any, the numbers and dates of the measures linked to the constraint.

If several constraints overlap at the same point, the profile gathers them in one entry **«PAI constraints»** with the number of constraints and a summary of the type «N overlapping constraints · most severe: [class] ([theme])». Each constraint opens by selecting it: «Select a constraint to see the details». The most severe class is chosen by comparing the number of the class.

Hovering or opening the popup shows the theme, the class, the place, the element at risk, the area and the length.

The same most severe class also appears in the «PAI constraints» box of the «Planning tools» section of the place profile.

Source written in the profile: Regione Siciliana, Autorità di Bacino, Piano di Assetto Idrogeologico (PAI), SITR; constraints in the Municipality of Palermo. The data is for information: for legal uses the official cartography of the Autorità di Bacino prevails.
