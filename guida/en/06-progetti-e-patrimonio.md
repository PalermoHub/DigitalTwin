# Public projects and heritage

## NRRP projects in Palermo

Keywords: NRRP, PNRR, Italia Domani, projects, mission, implementing body, CUP, funding, amounts, progress status.

Typical questions: Which NRRP projects are there in Palermo? How much money is coming? Who carries out a project?

The «NRRP projects» layer (Territory group) shows the projects of the National Recovery and Resilience Plan (PNRR) located in the city of Palermo only, from the Italia Domani open data (extraction of 13 June 2026): about 2,630 projects for about 921 million euro of PNRR funding.

On the map there are only the projects that have a place (about 1,280, grouped in ring markers divided into slices, one per mission, with the number of projects). The others (training, research, doctorates, networks such as tram and bus) have no place: they can be consulted in the profile and in the full list in CSV. The schools are positioned at their site (AgID IPA addresses).

The legend filters by mission, site and implementing body. In the profile, «NRRP» tab: each project with status (In Corso or Concluso), mission (M1…M7), implementing body clickable as a filter, amounts and link to the CUP on OpenCUP; the «NRRP in Palermo» section has totals and charts on all the projects, and a drop-down menu opens the projects of the Municipality and the University. Source: Italia Domani / Sogei (CC BY 4.0).

## Public works in Palermo (BDAP-MOP)

Keywords: public works, building sites, BDAP, MOP, State General Accounting Department, owner, sector, work phase, costs.

Typical questions: Which public works are there? How much does this work cost? Who is carrying it out?

The «Public works» layer (Territory group) shows the public works located in the city of Palermo only from the BDAP-MOP open data of the State General Accounting Department (Ragioneria generale dello Stato): about 1,350 works, with a planned cost of works of about 2.1 billion euro. Owners: Municipality, Metropolitan City, ANAS, IACP, health authorities and others.

On the map there are only the works for which an address was found (about 705): the others can be consulted in the profile, «Works» tab. The ANAS works are not on the map (addresses such as «SS 113»). The positions derive from the house numbers (there are no coordinates in the source), so they may be approximate.

The legend filters by 10 sectors, by works with a closed CUP and by owner. The profile gives owner, sector, work phase (derived from the actual dates: the source does not give progress), costs and declared funding. No totals on funding: there are anomalous values in the source. Source: Open BDAP, licence «CC BY – libero riuso citando la fonte». None of the works on the map coincides with a PNRR project (different CUPs).

## Properties declared to the MEF

Keywords: MEF, public properties, public assets, Treasury Department, property census, assets, cadastre.

Typical questions: Which public properties are there in Palermo? What does «declared to the MEF» mean?

The «Properties declared to the MEF» layer (Territory group) shows the real estate assets of the Municipality of Palermo declared in the census of the Ministry of Economy and Finance (Treasury Department, year 2023): about 8,000 assets. They are attached to buildings (about 1,260), to land (about 480 parcels) or to points. The icon is the euro.

The position of each asset is checked against the cadastre (sheet and parcel), then against the layers already mapped (municipal properties, schools, polling sites, offices, monuments), then against the declared position. The «Property declared to the MEF» profile lists the assets of the building. Source: MEF (CC BY 4.0).

## PEBA: architectural barriers

Keywords: PEBA, architectural barriers, accessibility, disability, wheelchair, critical issues, PDF form, routes, UNESCO.

Typical questions: Is a building or a street accessible? What does the PEBA score mean?

The «Architectural barriers» layer (Territory group) presents the Plan for the Elimination of Architectural Barriers of the Municipality of Palermo (Giunta Resolution no. 272 of 18/07/2026): 209 survey forms on administrative sites, schools, nurseries, green areas, streets, historic and UNESCO routes, squares and museums.

Each form has accessibility level, inaccessibility score, relevance, priority class, estimated cost, critical issues found, photos of the inspection (with a viewer) and PDF form. Buildings and green areas are polygons, the rest points with an icon for each group. OpenDataSicilia and PalermoHub processing (CC BY 4.0).

Warning: forms, texts and photos were extracted automatically from the original PDFs and the coordinates derive from the geocoding of the addresses: there may be errors or imprecise positioning.

## Former Noviziato dei Crociferi in 3D

Keywords: Crociferi, Noviziato, 3D model, floor plans, elevations, DWG, historic centre, rooms.

Typical questions: What is the Crociferi layer? Can I see the floor plans of the building?

The «Former Noviziato dei Crociferi in 3D» layer (Buildings group, **in development**) raises the floor plans of the building floor by floor: exploded levels, plan view with room numbers and elevations of the façades. The data come from the DWG plans, sections and elevations of the Municipality of Palermo, Historic Centre Department; the 3D model is a processing by the project.
