# «Zoning (zoning plan 2004)» and «Constraints» profiles

## «Zoning (zoning plan 2004)» profile

Keywords: zoning profile, zone, ZTO, description, scope, historic centre, historic net, PPE, instrument, zoning plan 2004, PRG 2004, land use plan, town plan.

Typical questions: Which zone of the zoning plan is this point in? What does «Scope: Historic centre» mean? Is the plan up to date?

When you click on the map, in the **«Planning tools»** tab the section **«Zoning (zoning plan 2004)»** appears, which brings together in a single section what on the plan are several maps. The rows, when the data exists:
- **Zone**: the code of the zone (ZTO).
- **Description** of the zone.
- **Scope**: «Historic net» and/or «Historic centre».
- **Instrument**: «PPE» (Piano Particolareggiato Esecutivo), only for the historic centre.

At the bottom there is the note: **«The current zoning plan is the 2004 General Variant: later variants may not be included.»** and the link «Cadastre, zoning plan and constraints on map». The section is marked as informational, with no legal value.

## «Constraints» profile

Keywords: constraints profile, area constraint, linear constraint, type, description, notes, zoning plan 2004, PRG 2004.

Typical questions: Which constraints are there on this point? What is the difference between an area constraint and a linear one?

If at the clicked point there are constraints of the 2004 zoning plan, in the **«Planning tools»** tab the section **«Constraints»** appears, with a group for each constraint:
- **Area constraint** and **Linear constraint**. If there is more than one, the groups are numbered (for example «Area constraint 1»).
- Each group has **Type**, **Description** and **Notes**, when present.

The same constraint is not repeated twice. At the bottom there is the link «Cadastre, zoning plan and constraints on map». The section is informational, with no legal value. The constraints of the hydrogeological plan are in a separate profile (see «PAI constraints»).
