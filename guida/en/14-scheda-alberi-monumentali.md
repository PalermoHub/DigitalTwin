# Profile «Monumental tree»

## What the profile of a monumental tree shows

Keywords: tree profile, monumental tree, homogeneous group, species, locality, trunk circumference, height, altitude, monumentality criteria, MASAF record.

Typical questions: What does the profile of a tree show? What does «homogeneous group» mean? What are the monumentality criteria?

When you click on a point of the «Monumental trees (MASAF)» layer, the profile has the name of the tree (or the species) as its title and, as its badge, **«Monumental tree»** or **«Homogeneous group»** (a group of trees indicated as a single element). The rows, when the data exists:
- **Species** and **Locality**.
- **Trunk circumference** in cm and **Height** in m. For homogeneous groups the labels become «Trunk circumference (max)» and «Height (max)».
- **Altitude** in m a.s.l.
- **Monumentality criteria**: the list of criteria given in the ministerial record.
- **Proposal for declaration of significant public interest**: «yes» or «no».
- **MASAF record**: the reference to the record in the ministerial list.

At the bottom there are the «MASAF list of monumental trees» link and the source: MASAF, list of the monumental trees of Italy (MASAF is the Ministry of Agriculture, Food Sovereignty and Forests). Clicking on a tree on the map also shows a popup with the same rows.
