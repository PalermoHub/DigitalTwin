# Traffic flow

## The «Traffic flow» layer

Keywords: traffic, queues, congestion, flow, speed, jammed roads, traffic jam, TomTom, free-flowing, slowed.

Typical questions: Is there traffic in Palermo right now? Where can I see the queues? How slow is traffic on via Libertà? How do I read the colour of the roads?

In the Layers panel, in the **«Transport»** group, section **«Road traffic»**, the **«Traffic flow»** layer colours Palermo's roads by **how freely traffic is moving right now**: **green** (free-flowing), **orange** (slowed), **red** (heavy), **dark red** (almost stopped). The colour depends on the ratio between the current speed and the speed with no traffic, so a road that is slow by nature does not look red if traffic moves as usual. The layer shows from **zoom 10** upwards and refreshes every **2 minutes** while it is on.

## The speed of a stretch

Keywords: click, popup, current speed, speed without traffic, delay, reliability.

Typical questions: How do I see the speed of a road? What does «delay on this stretch» mean? How reliable is the data?

With the layer on, a **click on a road** opens a card with the state of the stretch, the **current speed**, the **speed without traffic**, the **delay on the stretch** (shown only if at least 15 seconds) and the **reliability** of the data. If no road is detected near the click, nothing appears. A **closed** road is shown as such.

## Source and limits

Keywords: TomTom, update, source, licence, accuracy, cars, data.

Typical questions: Where does the data come from? How often is it updated? Is it official?

The data comes from **TomTom Traffic Flow**, built from millions of vehicles and connected devices; it is used under the free plan and must be credited («© TomTom», bottom right of the map). A service (Worker) renews it every 2 minutes for all users, and only for the Palermo area. The layer is **informative**: it is not official data from the Municipality, minor roads have fewer measurements and so lower reliability, and a stretch with no data may show no colour.
