# «Municipal property» profile

## What the Municipal property profile shows

Keywords: municipal property profile, properties of the Municipality, type, category, address, municipal assets, council-owned buildings.

Typical questions: Does this building belong to the Municipality? What does the profile of a municipal property show?

If the clicked point falls on a property of the «Municipal properties» layer (Territory group), the section **«Municipal property»** appears, with three rows:
- **Type**.
- **Category**.
- **Address**.

The data is that of the municipal properties of 2024 (Municipality of Palermo). The positions are the result of an **automatic georeferencing** and are purely indicative. For the public assets declared to the Ministry of Economy and Finance see the profile «Property declared to the MEF».
