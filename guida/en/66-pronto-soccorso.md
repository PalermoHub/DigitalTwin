# Emergency departments ("Health" group)

## What the "Emergency departments (waiting)" layer shows

Keywords: emergency department, ER, health, hospital, waiting, crowding, colour codes, triage, Villa Sofia, Cervello, Civico, Di Cristina, Policlinico, Ingrassia, Buccheri La Ferla, paediatric.

Typical questions: How crowded is the emergency department? Where are Palermo's emergency departments? What does the colour of the dot mean? How often is the data updated?

In the **Health** group of the "Layers" tab there is the **"Emergency departments (waiting)"** layer. It shows Palermo's **8 emergency departments**: Villa Sofia, Cervello (adult and paediatric), Civico, Di Cristina (paediatric), Policlinico, Ingrassia and Buccheri La Ferla. Each point has the hospital icon and a colour showing how crowded it is, based on the index published by the hospital (patients present compared with technical beds):
- **green**: within available places (up to 100%);
- **orange**: crowded (over 100% and up to 150%);
- **red**: very crowded (over 150%);
- **grey**: data not available or not up to date.

Data is re-read from the hospitals every 2 minutes. A value that is not published is not shown: it is never replaced by a zero.

## The emergency department card

Keywords: emergency department card, patients present, waiting, technical beds, red code, orange code, length of stay, 112.

Typical questions: What does the emergency department card show? What do the colour codes mean? Is the number a waiting time?

A click on a point, even with the layer off, opens the card in the "Place" tab. It shows, when the data exists:
- **Facility**: the hospital or health authority, whether adult or paediatric, address and, if known, phone.
- **Situation now**: crowding as a percentage, patients present, patients waiting, technical beds and the time of the last update.
- **Patients by colour code**: for red, orange, blue, green and white, how many are waiting, in treatment and under observation, and the average wait where the hospital provides it.
- **Length of stay of patients present**: under 24 hours, 24 to 48 hours, over 48 hours.
- **«Patients waiting, last 24 hours» chart**: a line with one value every half hour (average of the readings); where the hospital published nothing there is a gap, never an invented zero. Touching the chart shows time and value. It shows whether the queue is growing or shrinking; it is not a waiting time. The «i» icon explains what it shows, how it works and how to read it.
- In the «Structure» group: the address is a link that opens **directions** in Google Maps (in the navigation app on a phone), then the **official website** and the **data source page** the waiting figures are read from.

If the data is old the card says so and the point turns grey. **Note**: the figure counts patients; it does not promise a waiting time. In an emergency call **112** (118 for medical emergencies).

Sources: the hospitals (Ospedali Riuniti Villa Sofia-Cervello, ARNAS Civico, AOUP Policlinico, ASP Palermo, Fatebenefratelli), collected by the PalermoHub app "palermo-ps". When two emergency departments are in the same building (Cervello adult and paediatric) the icons sit side by side.
