# «NRRP» tab and PNRR project profiles

## Where the PNRR projects are in the profile

Keywords: NRRP tab, PNRR tab, PNRR profile, PNRR projects, implementing body, projects without a place, summary, drop-down menu, Municipality and University.

Typical questions: Where do I find the details of a PNRR project? How do I see the projects that are not on the map? What is in the NRRP tab?

The details of the PNRR projects are in the **NRRP** tab of the right-hand profile. The tab has three parts, in this order:
1. **«All Municipality and University projects»**: two drop-down menus, «1 · Choose the body» and «2 · Choose the project». These bodies have many projects, some with no point on the map: you choose the body and then the project to see its profile. The badge is the number of projects in the menu.
2. **The profile of the project clicked** (one for each project chosen on the map).
3. **«NRRP in Palermo»**: the summary with numbers and charts on all the projects. The badge is the year the data was extracted.

On the map there are only the projects that have a place; the others (training, research, scholarships, networks…) can be consulted in the NRRP tab and in the full list in CSV. The hover box on grouped markers invites you to click «for the list and more information»; the popup points to the profile, NRRP tab. A marker can group several projects at the same point («N NRRP projects» or «Several addresses at the same spot»).

## The profile of a single PNRR project

Keywords: mission, component, measure, status, implementing body, PNRR funding, total cost, planned start, actual end, CUP, OpenCUP.

Typical questions: What does «M2» mean? What is the PNRR funding? Who is carrying out the project? How do I see the CUP?

The profile has the project title as its title and, as its badge, «PNRR» with the mission (for example «PNRR M2»). The rows, when the data exists:
- **Mission** with its name, **Component**, **Measure**.
- **Status**: Completed or In progress, as declared in the source.
- **Implementing body**: a click filters the map on the projects of that body.
- **NRRP funding** and **Total cost**.
- **Planned start**, **Actual start**, **Planned end**, **Actual end**.
- **Nature**, **Type**, **Sector**.
- **Address**, **Seat of the implementing body**, **School (Schools layer)** and **Position**: «Address (street number)», «Street only: approximate position» or «Seat of the implementing body: approximate position».
- **CUP**: the value is a link to the project page on OpenCUP.

Below appears the project description, then the links «Italia Domani open data catalogue», «Project locations (CSV)» and «Palermo NRRP on Cruscotto Italia». At the bottom the source: Italia Domani, Struttura di missione PNRR (NRRP Mission Unit), Sogei, open data catalogue (CC BY 4.0).

## The PNRR missions

Keywords: missions, M1, M2, M3, M4, M5, M6, M7, REPowerEU, mission colours.

Typical questions: What are the missions M1-M7?

In the app there are seven missions, each with a colour (the slices of the ring of the markers):
- **M1** Digitalisation, innovation, competitiveness, culture and tourism.
- **M2** Green revolution and ecological transition.
- **M3** Infrastructure for sustainable mobility.
- **M4** Education and research.
- **M5** Inclusion and cohesion.
- **M6** Health.
- **M7** REPowerEU.

The legend filters by mission, seat and implementing body. The hover box shows the title, mission and status, PNRR funding, implementing body and address.

## The «NRRP in Palermo» summary

Keywords: PNRR summary, NRRP summary, totals, charts, funding by mission, progress status, projects by year, sectors, implementing bodies, download CSV.

Typical questions: How many PNRR projects are there? How many are completed? Can I download the list?

The summary shows three boxes: **Projects** (number), **NRRP funding** and **Completed** (percentage). Then it says how many projects are on the map out of the total («the others have no address and are in the full list»). The charts:
- Funding by mission.
- Progress status.
- Projects by planned end year (with a note on the projects with no date).
- Sectors.
- Implementing bodies (a click on a body filters the map on its projects).

At the bottom there are the «Download the full list (CSV)» link and the note: projects located only in the city of Palermo; status, amounts and dates are those declared in the source (with the extraction date) and **have not been checked on the ground**.
