# Charts and rankings of the profile: population, trend, commuting

## Ranking of residents («Census section» profile)

Keywords: residents ranking, district, neighbourhood, UPL, Italians, foreigners, trend 2021-2023, residents per unit, total Palermo, population ranking, inhabitants.

Typical questions: How do I see the inhabitants of my neighbourhood? What does the arrow ▲ next to the number mean? How do I switch between districts, neighbourhoods and UPL?

In the «Census section» profile («Population» tab) there is a **ranking of residents** titled «Resident population by [level]». At the top three buttons choose the level: **District**, **Neighbourhoods**, **UPL** (the one chosen is kept even when you reopen the profile).

The list is ordered from the most populous. Each row has: position, name, a bar divided into **Italians** and **foreigners**, the number of residents, the percentage of foreigners and a **trend badge 2021→2023**: ▲ growth, ▼ decline, ≈ stable (change below 0.5%), with the percentage. Hovering over the badge you read «2021: [n] → 2023: [n]». Hovering over a row you read total, Italians, foreigners and percentage of foreigners. The unit in which the clicked point falls is highlighted and brought into view in the list.

At the bottom there is the total «Palermo — total» with the residents, the foreigners (for.) and their percentage, with the trend. Source written in the profile: ISTAT, Permanent Census 2021, census sections; trend: 2023 update, AGID Cruscotto Italia (dati.gov.it).

## The trend chart («Population trend» profile)

Keywords: trend chart, inhabitants 2011 2021 2023, scenario 2030 2035, uncertainty band, solid line, dashed line, trend scenario, population projection.

Typical questions: How do I read the chart? What does the dashed line mean? What is the coloured area?

For each level (UPL, Neighbourhood, District) the profile has a collapsible section, «[Level]: [name]» (the first is open), with a chart:
- **Solid line** with the observed points ’11, ’21 and ’23 (filled circles).
- **Dashed line** with the scenario ’30 and ’35 (empty circles) and a **coloured area** that is the uncertainty band between minimum and maximum.
- The number of inhabitants is written on each point (below the point for 2023).
- The colour follows the change 2011-2021: green growth, purple decline.

Below the chart: «Trend scenario to 2035: [central value] ([minimum]–[maximum])». At the bottom the collapsible section «Note on data quality», closed at the start, with the caution on the comparison between the censuses.

## Boxes and rankings of commuting

Keywords: lives and works here, works elsewhere, jobs filled by commuters, working population, jobs, net balance, top destinations, top origins, show all links, commuting rankings.

Typical questions: What do the three boxes mean? What is the net balance? Why are there only 25 municipalities?

The drawn part of the «Commuting for work» profile has:
- **Three percentage boxes**: «Lives and works here», «Works elsewhere» and «Jobs filled by commuters».
- **Three key figures**: «Working population», «Jobs» and «Net balance» (with the sign + or −, highlighted differently if positive or negative).
- A hint: «The map shows the top 25 links per direction (share of at least 1%). Turn on “Show all links” in the legend to see them all.»
- Two rankings with bars: **«Top destinations (outbound)»** and **«Top origins (inbound)»**, with municipality, number of people and share of the direction.
- The collapsible section «Note on data quality» (data at 31 December 2021 in the middle of the pandemic; municipalities at the centroid, arcs are not real routes).
