# The three videos that explain the app

## Which videos there are

Keywords: video, tutorial, app tour, YouTube, video guide, how to use, explanation, film, walkthrough.

Typical questions: Are there any videos that explain the app? Where do I find the video tutorials? How many videos are there? How do I use the map, is there a video?

There are **three videos**, titled «Palermo Digital Twin: ...». The videos themselves are available only in Italian; the titles below are the English titles shown in the app.

- **Video 1**, «How to use the map with Palermo's open data»: https://youtu.be/H8oNAYFxHeQ
- **Video 2**, «The national RNDT catalogue on the map (openrndt-geolibre plugin)»: https://youtu.be/uP7hDqpVDNw
- **Video 3**, «Geoimage, the historical map over today's Palermo»: https://youtu.be/CADYohEEGhM

In the app the short names are «How to use the map», «The RNDT catalogue on the map» and «Geoimage, the historical map». The presentation page describes them like this: «Three videos: the map, the RNDT plugin and Geoimage, step by step.»

## Where the videos are in the app

Keywords: where are the videos, guide, Info, presentation, The app tour, thumbnails, video window, Watch the videos.

Typical questions: Where do the videos open? How do I watch the videos inside the app? Where is «The app tour»?

The videos are in two places:

- **Guide (Info sheet)**: below the guide index the three thumbnails appear with the Play button and the short name. A click opens the video in a **floating window**, like Street View: it is moved by dragging the bar and resized from the corner.
- **Presentation** (page `presentazione.html`): the **«The app tour»** section shows the same three videos; the **«Watch the videos»** button at the top leads to this section.

The videos are on YouTube in «enhanced privacy» mode: the browser contacts YouTube only if you press Play (see `51-pagine-privacy-lingua-e-offline.md`).

## Which video to watch

Keywords: map video, RNDT video, Geoimage video, openrndt-geolibre plugin, historical map, which video.

Typical questions: Which video explains the map? Is there a video on the RNDT catalogue? Is there a video on Geoimage? Where do I see how to overlay a historical map?

- **General use of the map and of the open data**: video 1, «How to use the map». The written text is in the files `01-uso-della-mappa.md` and `45-barra-strati-legende-e-ordine.md`.
- **Adding data from the national RNDT catalogue**: video 2, «The RNDT catalogue on the map» (openrndt-geolibre plugin). The written text is in `49-catalogo-rndt.md`.
- **Overlaying a historical map on today's Palermo**: video 3, «Geoimage, the historical map». The written text is in `50-geoimage.md`.

If the video is not enough, the texts of this guide explain the same functions step by step.
