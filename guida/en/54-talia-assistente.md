# Talia, the app's assistant

## Who Talia is

Keywords: Talia, chat bot, assistant, chatbot, what is your name, name, who are you, virtual assistant.

Typical questions: What is your name? Who is Talia? What is the Chat bot? Who am I talking to?

**Talia** is the app's assistant: it answers questions about the app, its layers and its functions. The name is «Talia»; in Sicilian «talìa» means «look». The answers do not come from an external service: they are drawn from the site's guide, in the language of the app, and above each answer is the title of the entry it comes from.

---

## How to open it and how to use it

Keywords: open Talia, Talia tab, speech-bubble button, right-hand bar, text field, Send, bin, Read more.

Typical questions: Where do I find Talia? How do I ask Talia a question? How do I clear the conversation?

The speech-bubble button, in the toolbar at the top right, or the «Talia» tab of the right-hand bar open Talia. You type the question in the field at the bottom and press «Send»; on opening, Talia also offers a few questions to tap. «Read more» expands a long answer; if it finds nothing, Talia says so and suggests similar questions; the bin button clears the conversation.

## What it can do besides answering

Keywords: buttons below the answer, switch on a layer, filters, card, guided paths, open the guide.

Typical questions: Can Talia switch on a layer? What are the buttons below the answer? Can it open the card of a place?

Below the answer there are buttons that let you act without searching through the menus: switch a layer on or off, choose a filter, open the card of a place that was found, open a tab of the panels, start a guided path or open the linked entry of the guide. Details on the filters in `01-uso-della-mappa.md` and in `44-ricerca-e-filtri.md`.

## Searching for an address and seeing what is nearby

Keywords: address, street, house number, what is nearby, surroundings, near me, Talia address.

Typical questions: Can Talia search for a street and house number? What is near via Libertà 12? How do I see what is around an address?

Type a street with its house number (for example «via Libertà 12») or the name of a street («piazza Politeama») in Talia's field. Talia offers a button for each address found: tapping it moves the map to the point and opens the card, with the place's data and the points of the layers that lie nearby, within the radius of the neighbours. The summary of the card then appears at the top of Talia. If the house number does not exist, Talia suggests the street and says so.
