# Talia, the app's assistant

## Who Talia is

Keywords: Talia, chat bot, assistant, chatbot, what is your name, name, who are you, virtual assistant.

Typical questions: What is your name? Who is Talia? What is the Chat bot? Who am I talking to?

**Talia** is the app's assistant: it answers questions about the app, its layers and its functions. The name is «Talia»; in Sicilian «talìa» means «look». The answers do not come from an external service: they are drawn from the site's guide, in the language of the app, and above each answer is the title of the entry it comes from.

---

## How to open it and how to use it

Keywords: open Talia, Talia tab, speech-bubble button, right-hand bar, text field, Send, bin, Read more.

Typical questions: Where do I find Talia? How do I ask Talia a question? How do I clear the conversation?

The speech-bubble button, in the toolbar at the top right, or the «Talia» tab of the right-hand bar open Talia. You type the question in the field at the bottom and press «Send»; on opening, Talia also offers a few questions to tap. «Read more» expands a long answer; if it finds nothing, Talia says so and suggests similar questions; the bin button clears the conversation.

## What it can do besides answering

Keywords: buttons below the answer, switch on a layer, filters, card, guided paths, open the guide.

Typical questions: Can Talia switch on a layer? What are the buttons below the answer? Can it open the card of a place?

Below the answer there are buttons that let you act without searching through the menus: switch a layer on or off, choose a filter, open the card of a place that was found, open a tab of the panels, start a guided path or open the linked entry of the guide. Details on the filters in `01-uso-della-mappa.md` and in `44-ricerca-e-filtri.md`.

## Searching for an address and seeing what is nearby

Keywords: address, street, house number, what is nearby, surroundings, near me, Talia address.

Typical questions: Can Talia search for a street and house number? What is near via Libertà 12? How do I see what is around an address?

Type a street with its house number (for example «via Libertà 12») or the name of a street («piazza Politeama») in Talia's field. Talia offers a button for each address found: tapping it moves the map to the point and opens the card, with the place's data and the points of the layers that lie nearby, within the radius of the neighbours. The summary of the card then appears at the top of Talia; to have the card described in plain words, tap Talia's icon in the card. If the house number does not exist, Talia suggests the street and says so.

## Searching for places, zones, fires and parcels

Keywords: search, find, offices, monuments, PNRR, public works, district, circoscrizione, UPL, fires, PAI, landslides, parcel, sheet, cadastre, Talia search.

Typical questions: Can Talia search for an office or a monument? How do I find a district or a circoscrizione? How do I search for a cadastral parcel with Talia? Does Talia find fires or PAI constraints?

Talia searches by name for schools, polling stations, monuments, trees, fountains, stops and lines, stations, charging points, fuel stations, offices, properties, PEBA cards, PNRR projects and public works (the CUP is typed like a line number): each result has a button that opens its card. It can also take the map to a district, a circoscrizione or a UPL, to fires (also for one year, for example «fires 2023») and to PAI constraints («landslides», «PAI»), and to a cadastral parcel written as «sheet 12 parcel 345» or «12/345»: here the button, «Go to: …», moves the map and applies the filter without opening a card. It does not search buildings (3D), heat islands or ISPRA geology by name: for those, ask for the layer and Talia offers to switch it on.

## Questions Talia answers from the data

Keywords: filter, above, below, amount, closest, near me, how many, how many residents, most populous, what am I looking at, layers on, location, Talia data.

Typical questions: What is the closest charging point to me? How many drinking fountains are there? Which district is the most populous? How many residents does Zisa have? What am I looking at on the map? Which layers are on?

Some questions Talia answers from the app's data, without going through the guide. «The closest charging point to me» (it also works for fuel stations, stops, fountains, schools, offices, monuments, trees and polling stations) reads your position, once the browser has your consent, and offers the three closest points with their distance: a button takes the map to the point and opens its card. «How many drinking fountains are there?» gives the total in the data. «Most populous district» and «how many residents does Zisa have» use the residents ranking of the latest year available (also for UPLs and circoscrizioni). «What am I looking at?» lists the layers that are on, each with a button to switch it off. Some questions apply a filter to the map: «PNRR above 1 million», «works below 500 thousand euro», «fatal crashes 2022». Talia switches the layer on, filters it and shows a «Remove the filter» button (for PNRR and works the filter also appears in the layer's legend); for crashes year and severity apply, as in the Filters panel. Counts for a single zone («how many schools in Zisa») do not exist yet: Talia then answers from the guide.

## Having Talia describe a place

Keywords: plain words, describe, explain the card, Talia icon, terrain, OMI market, population, how to ask, examples, question mark.

Typical questions: How do I get Talia to explain a card? What does the Talia icon in the card do? How do I know what I can ask Talia? Where are the example questions?

In a place's card, next to copy and print, there is Talia's icon: tap it and Talia opens and describes the place in plain sentences, one part for each tab (Market, Population, Terrain). The sentences are computed from the card's numbers with stated rules, without artificial intelligence: «how it works» shows the number and the threshold that produced the sentence, and «values without a sentence» lists those close to normal, where there is nothing to say. OMI quotations are for a zone, not the price of a property. In Talia's header the question mark «?» opens «What you can ask me»: examples by type (finding a place, near you, numbers, filters, understanding the app) that you try with a tap.
