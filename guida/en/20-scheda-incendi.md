# «Fire» profile

## What the profile of a fire shows

Keywords: fire profile, wildfire, date, locality, starting place, type of event, total area, wooded, non-wooded, land use, intervention, duration, AIB crews, firefighting cost, injured, deaths.

Typical questions: What does the profile of a fire show? How long did the intervention last? What are the AIB crews?

When you click on an area of the «Fires» layer, the profile is titled «Fire at [locality]» (or «Fire [year]» if the locality is missing) and has as its badge «Fire [year]». The groups, with only the rows that have a value:

**General data**
- **Date** (day/month/year), **Locality**, **Starting place**, **Type of event**.

**Areas**
- **Total area**, **Wooded area**, **Non-wooded area**, **Other forest areas**, all in hectares (ha).
- **Land use** and **Scorch height**.

**Intervention**
- **End of intervention** and **Duration of intervention** (in hours and minutes).
- **AIB crews**.
- **Firefighting cost** in euro.
- **Injured** and **Deaths**.

If several fires overlap at the same point, the profile gathers them in one entry **«Fires»** with the number and a summary («N overlapping fires · from [year] to [year]»); each fire opens by selecting it. The popup on the map shows title, date and the three areas (total, wooded, non-wooded).

Source written in the profile: Regione Siciliana, Corpo Forestale, Censimento Incendi (SIF), perimeters of the areas burnt by fire in the Municipality of Palermo.
