# Three-year public works plan 2026-2028: layer and «Three-year plan» tab

## The Three-year plan layer on the map

Keywords: three-year plan, three-year programme, public works 2026-2028, council resolution 553/2026, projects, annual list, Three-year plan layer, legend, sector.

Typical questions: What is the «Three-year public works plan 2026-2028» layer? What do the colours of the points mean? How do I filter projects by year or sector? Why are some projects not on the map?

The layer shows the **Three-year public works programme 2026-2028 of the City of Palermo** (council resolution no. 553/2026): **548 projects worth 4.37 billion euro**. It sits in the «Territory» group, in the same sub-group as the public works (BDAP-MOP). The tables were extracted from the City's PDFs and the geometries traced from the programme maps with the help of Claude AI: the data are informational and have no legal value.

The map shows **points, lines and polygons** coloured by year: red = 1st year (2026), orange = 2nd (2027), yellow = 3rd (2028), grey = approximate position. **454 projects out of 548** have at least one geometry; the other **94** (about 2 billion euro, including the metro) have none and can only be found in the side panel.

The legend under the layer works as a filter: the **year** boxes (plus «Approximate position») and the **sector** boxes (Transport, Environment and water resources, Energy, Productive areas, Social infrastructure, Tourism, trade and research, Other services). The filter also applies to the side panel: hidden projects do not appear in the tab. On hover the tooltip shows title, sector, three-year amount and neighbourhood. The «data table» tool (top-right toolbar) opens the list of projects, with amount, year, CUP and neighbourhood, and it can be downloaded.

## The «Three-year plan» tab of the side panel

Keywords: Three-year plan tab, project profile, programme amounts, funding sources, annual list, candidate CUP, look up a project, summary, charts.

Typical questions: Where do I find the details of a project of the three-year plan? How do I open a project that is not on the map? What does «candidate CUP» mean? Where do I see the totals of the programme?

The details are in the **Three-year plan** tab of the right-hand panel (also with the layer off: just click on the map). The tab has three parts, in this order:
1. **Look up a project of the programme**: a two-step drop-down, «1 · Choose the sector» and «2 · Choose the project». Each entry shows code, title and amount; projects without a geometry are marked «not on the map». The badge is the number of projects (548).
2. **The profile of the clicked project** (one for each project under the click or nearby, within the nearby-points radius).
3. **Three-year programme 2026-2028**: the summary with figures and charts on the whole programme. The badge is the total amount.

## The profile of a project

Keywords: project code, sector, sub-sector, type, priority, RUP, CUI, CUP, CUP status, planned award, amounts per year, private capital, funding sources, annual list, candidate CUP.

Typical questions: What does «CUP missing: not yet requested» mean? What is the annual list? Why is the candidate CUP only a proposal?

The title is the project's, and the badge shows its three-year amount. Rows appear only when the data exist:

**Project data**: code, sector and sub-sector, type, priority, RUP (single project manager), planned award year, functional lot and complex work, CUI, **CUP** (the value is a link to the OpenCUP page), district, neighbourhood and UPL. When the CUP is missing the status says why: «below the 150,000 € threshold, not mandatory» or «not yet requested, work only planned» (classification derived from the amount).

**Programme amounts**: cost of the first, second and third year (2026, 2027, 2028), three-year total, later years, total amount, private capital, value of properties, last loan instalment due.

**Funding sources** (for the 91 projects that have them): type of resource, funding description, amount.

**2026 annual list** (91 projects): annual amount, purpose, design level, planning compliance, environmental constraints check, contracting authority and AUSA code.

**Candidate CUP (proposal to be checked)**: for projects without a CUP, a CUP found in the OpenCUP dataset by description and amount, with a reliability level. **The official CUP has not been changed**: the candidate is only a suggestion to check.

## The programme summary

Keywords: summary, charts, amount per year, by sector, by type, by district, resources, ten most expensive projects.

Typical questions: How much is the programme worth each year? Which are the most expensive projects? Where do I see the projects in a table?

At the bottom of the tab: three boxes (projects, three-year amount, projects in the 2026 annual list), the note on how many projects are on the map, and bar charts: **amount per year** (2028 weighs more than half, because of large works such as the metro), **projects and amount by sector**, **types of intervention**, **by district** (only projects with a geometry), **resources by type** (earmarked revenue, loan, private capital, budget, other) and **the ten most expensive projects**. The guided path «Public works in the pipeline» switches on the Three-year plan, public works, NRRP and neighbourhoods together.
