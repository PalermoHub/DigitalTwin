# Buildings and population

## Buildings and 3D buildings

Keywords: buildings, built-up area, footprints, 3D, height, building use, population per building.

Typical questions: How do I see the buildings? Can I see them in 3D? How many inhabitants does a building have?

The «Buildings» group has the «Built-up area» layer (from zoom 12), with the footprints of about 111,800 buildings (volumetric units of the Municipal Technical Map), and «3D buildings», which extrudes them. The place profile shows the building with its use. The population per building is an **estimate**. In 3D view the buildings of the point layers (monuments, schools, polling sites, municipal offices, municipal properties, MEF, PEBA) are coloured.

## Inhabitants by census section

Keywords: population, inhabitants, residents, census section, ISTAT, permanent census, density, indicators.

Typical questions: How many inhabitants does my neighbourhood have? Where do the data come from?

The «Population by section» layer (Population group) shows the residents by census section from the ISTAT permanent census (2021 and 2023). The values by section are **sample estimates**, not exact counts. In 2023 the sections are 3,090 against the 3,600 of 2021. In the «Population» tab the «Census section» card gives the ISTAT code of the section and the indicators calculated for the selected year, a ranking with district, neighbourhood and UPL, and the links to «Demographic exploration», ANNCUS and the PalermoHub bivariate maps (density × public transport supply, house number density × population, population exposed to hydrogeological risk).

## Population trend 2011-2023

Keywords: trend, population trend, decline, growth, 2011, 2021, 2023, projection, 2030, 2035, depopulation.

Typical questions: Is Palermo's population shrinking? Which neighbourhood is growing? What will happen by 2035?

The «Population trend 2011-2023» layer (Population group) shows how the population of UPL, neighbourhoods and districts changes between the 2011, 2021 and 2023 censuses (ISTAT). The colour is the percentage change (purple decline, green growth). The level (UPL, Neighbourhood, District) and the symbol (pill with the percentage or line chart) are chosen from the legend.

ISTAT totals: 2011 about 657,600 inhabitants, 2021 about 635,400, 2023 about 630,400. The chart includes a **trend scenario** to 2030 and 2035: it is a simple projection, with an uncertainty band, not a cohort demographic model. For the Municipality the central projection to 2035 is about 606,000 inhabitants (range 593,700-614,400).

Cautions: the traditional 2011 census and the permanent 2021/2023 census are not fully comparable; a decline over two years is not a trend. Strong increases in the historic centre (the UPL «Tribunali o Kalsa» is at +27.3 % between 2011 and 2021, the I district at +14.8 %) are to be read as repopulation, with caution because of the change of method. The place profile, in the «Population» tab, shows the charts of the three levels for the point.

## Commuting to and from Palermo (2021)

Keywords: commuting, home-work trips, commuters, ISTAT 2021, inbound, outbound, balance, connected municipalities.

Typical questions: How many people come to work in Palermo? Where do the people of Palermo go to work?

The «Commuting to and from Palermo 2021» layer (Population group) shows the home-work trips between Palermo and the other municipalities (ISTAT, 2021 Census): who goes out, who comes in and how many both live and work in the city, with the rankings of the connected municipalities. For the municipality clicked, the profile gives: Inbound (work in Palermo), Outbound (Palermo residents) and Balance (inbound minus outbound). PalermoHub processing (CC BY 4.0).
