# «Stop», «Station» and «Lines» profiles

## Profile of a stop or a station

Keywords: stop profile, station, metro, bus stop, tram, lines that serve it, wheelchair accessible, opening soon, next departures.

Typical questions: What does the profile of a stop show? What does «Opening soon» mean? How do I see whether it is wheelchair accessible?

When you click on a stop, the profile has the name as its title and as its badge **«Stop»** (bus and tram) or **«Station»** (metro and railway), with the icon of the bus or of the train. The rows, when the data exists:
- **Status**: «Opening soon» for the stations not yet open.
- **Lines**: the list of the lines that serve it.
- **Wheelchair accessible**.

Below the rows the profile has a dynamic part with the lines that serve the stop and the next departures (see the guide «Stops of bus, tram and metro lines»). Hovering over a stop, the tooltip shows the name and the list of the lines; for stations that are opening soon it says so.

## Profile of the lines

Keywords: lines profile, line, directions, tram, bus, metro, line number, line colour.

Typical questions: How do I see a line? What is in the «Lines» box?

When you click on the route of a line (or on a stop), the profile shows an entry **«Line [number]»** if there is only one line, or **«N lines»** if there are more than one. The badge gives the type: Tram, Metro or Bus. Each line has its directions and its colour. Hovering over the route, the tooltip shows the first four lines (number, name and colour) and «others» if there are more.

In the box of a line the **«Stops»** block opens: the stops appear in sequence like a strip diagram, with the interchanges with the other lines (see the guide on public transport).
