# «Municipal offices» profile

## What the profile of an office site shows

Keywords: offices profile, site, area, sector, operational unit, U.O., manager, contacts, telephone numbers, email, offices and managers, municipal offices.

Typical questions: What is in the profile of a municipal site? How do I find the manager of an office? What is a U.O.?

When you click on a site of the «Municipal offices (sites)» layer, the profile has the name of the site as its title and «Municipal offices» as its badge. The rows:
- **Address**.
- **Offices**: how many offices are based there.
- **Areas**: in how many areas of the Municipality they are organised.

Below there is the accordion list **«Offices and managers (N)»**: one entry for each area, with the number of offices. When opened, it lists the offices of the area from the highest level to the lowest: first the **Area**, then the **Sectors**, then the **U.O.** (operational units). For each office the name is a link to its page on the Municipality's website and next to it appear the level (Area or Sector), the **manager** and the **contacts** (telephone numbers and email).

The popup on the map shows name, address, the number of offices and the four largest areas with the number of offices, plus «other areas» if there are any.

Source written in the profile: website of the Municipality of Palermo (comune.palermo.it/amministrazione/uffici). The official contacts are those on the Municipality's website.
